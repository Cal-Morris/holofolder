# Content calendar, November 2026 to February 2027

Dates come from [channels-and-automation.md](channels-and-automation.md#3-timely-hooks-nov-2026-to-mar-2027) and [competitors-and-keywords.md](competitors-and-keywords.md#current-set-calendar-english). Anything marked "unconfirmed" must be checked against pokemon.com before we publish a date, because we never state unverified facts as if they were confirmed. The growth loop works from this table every week and adds new hooks as they are announced.

The weekly rhythm sits underneath the dated hooks. Monday is a price movers post (high or medium confidence only). Wednesday is the showcase binder of the week. Friday is a feature or changelog post when something has shipped, and otherwise a binder layout tip. Sunday is the Self Promo Sunday draft for Cal to post on r/PokemonTCG. Instagram gets carousels on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
