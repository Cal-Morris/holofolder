# Marketing

This folder holds the public, durable parts of Holo Folder's marketing: how we position the product, how we write, and the SEO audit of our own site. Working material that names real people, communities, competitors or business numbers (the detailed growth plan, content calendar, competitor and channel research, creator outreach and the weekly scoreboard) lives in `marketing/private/`, which is gitignored and stays on Cal's machine.

## Strategy in brief

We aim to win the UK collector who keeps a physical binder: Holo Folder shows your actual binder pages, priced in pounds, with a mode built for the card show floor. The free plan has unlimited cards in one binder plus card and page scanning; Premium adds more binders, partner collections, price trends and signals, analytics, and Show mode deal checks.

Search is the engine that compounds, through public set, Pokémon and card pages built from our card catalogue. Social posts (Instagram, TikTok and X), community participation and small creators bring the first users and the brand signals search needs. Sharing and invites turn every collector into a channel. Every link points at something a visitor can use without signing up first.

## What lives here

- [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md) holds the voice, claims rules and account handles that every published word must follow.
- [seo-audit.md](seo-audit.md) is the technical and content audit of holofolder.com, with prioritised fixes.
- [social/](social/README.md) holds the script that renders every social image at each platform's size from the showcase binders and our card mirror. Use it rather than reposting blog artwork.

The [growth-loop skill](../.agents/skills/growth-loop/SKILL.md) runs the plan week to week from the private files.

## Rules that never change

We never invent card data, prices, dates or social proof. Price signals are shown only at high or medium confidence. Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups, replies, DMs and creator contact stay human. Anything naming real people goes in `marketing/private/`, never in a commit.
