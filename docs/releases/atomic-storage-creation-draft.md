# Unpublished: named collections with copied starting lists

Draft only. Do not publish until #396 native creation recovery, #379 runtime/API/offline/compatibility gates and production verification pass. Slab choices additionally require #390. This internal backend slice is not available in the app or public API.

Create a named binder or custom storage collection with an editable starting list. Start blank, choose a Dex or generation, select Pokémon, or copy actual catalog printings in your chosen order. Targets describe what you are collecting; they do not add owned cards or use your saved-card allowance. Editing your collection never changes another collection or its source preset.

Names, locations and collection contents stay within your account and linked partner unless you explicitly enable public sharing. Existing collection-count and custom-layout plan rules apply. Creation requires a connection and retries preserve the original result.
