# Unpublished enabled-release draft: Start with your collection

Status: staged UI only, do not publish or announce as shipped. Related #367/#368; enablement #379.

Choose a blank binder, National Dex, a generation, selected Pokémon or individual catalog printings. Preview the targets in order before creating your collection. Want Maushold, Tandemaus and Snorlax together? Pick the species you collect. Building a special Pikachu collection? Choose the actual printings from the catalog, with their set and language shown.

Your starting arrangement stays yours to edit. Add free pockets, remove targets or change the order as your collection changes. Progress follows the targets you have now; extra cards in free pockets are counted separately. A blank binder starts empty.

Atomic creation, durable replay/ACK and user-facing layout editing are not delivered by this UI slice.
