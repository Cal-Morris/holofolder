Proposed title: Organize your collection in binders or custom storage

Proposed copy after enablement verification: Choose Binder or Custom storage when starting a collection. Give it a name, optionally add a private location for Custom, then start blank or copy an editable target arrangement. Custom opens in List and Binder opens in Binder; you can change the view later. Targets do not add owned copies or grades. Both kinds use your existing collection allowance. A linked partner shares the owner's storage, while public sharing remains a separate opt-in.
