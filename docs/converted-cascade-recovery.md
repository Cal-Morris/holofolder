## Supported whole events

Genuine 073 conversion followed by 074 mapped-slot removal or 075 `removeSlots` / `deleteCollection` can now be recovered when all evidence and current dependencies pass. It restores the original collection/slot IDs, saved order, targets, wants and their people, copied preset/config, metadata/grid/default flags and complete conversion receipt/authority. Versions advance rather than rewind. A replacement native default is checked and restored as part of the same event. Deletion leaves the exact owned copies Unassigned. Recovery checks their retained versions/evidence and restores only eligible placements. It does not INSERT those inventory items, change their acquisition time, create another copy, or call acquisition/outbox/search APIs. Duplicate printings, species-only unknowns and certificates remain distinct. A newer same-copy edit/move/removal, ABA, missing source, changed receipt, occupied stable ID/position, name collision or changed replacement default rejects the entire event with no plan token. Unrelated newer partner work survives a fresh preview. No partial inverse, forced overwrite or displaced card exists.

The explicit Unassigned alternative remains limited to 077/080's isolated removed-owned-copy recovery. It is deliberately **not** extended to collection deletion or mapped-slot cascades. Those choices return `invalid_unassigned_recovery`; deleting a collection did not remove ownership in the first place.
