---
title: Scanning into your binder
description: Scan a binder page or a single card, choose where each card goes, and add them in one save.
---

Open the binder's **Storage** menu and choose **Scan page** or **Scan card**. Both need a connection, a complete current read of your collection, no unsynced edits and a saved page grid that matches the binder. Until then the entries stay disabled.

## Scan a page

Choose **Take photo**, or **Or choose a photo** when the camera isn't available. Each detected pocket is matched to the pocket at that position on the page you scanned. A card being present in a pocket does not identify it or prove you own it, so for each card choose **Choose card** and pick the catalog card from **Possible matches**, or **Skip** it. Cards already recorded in that pocket show **Already in your binder. Nothing to add.** Trainers and Energy work the same way as Pokémon.

## Scan a card

**Scan card** keeps the existing on-device text reading and cached lookup. **Type instead** searches card identities already read or cached on this device. Unknown cards stay unresolved. Scanning never reads grades, certificates or label artwork.

## Review and add

For each card, answer **Which card is this?**, **Which version?** and **Which copy?** where they apply. You can use one of your recorded spare copies or add **An ungraded copy**. Under **Put it in**, pick a matching pocket or **A new pocket at the end**. Two cards cannot go into the same pocket, and a card that doesn't fit a pocket's target is refused. When everything is chosen, confirm **Add N cards**.

All the cards from one review are saved as a single atomic inventory request, in the order shown. A batch that exceeds the request limits is refused whole, so scan a smaller page. Nothing is padded into pockets you didn't create, and no ownership is implied for skipped cells. Adding an ungraded copy is unavailable when only graded matching spare copies exist; choose one of those copies instead.

If the binder, its arrangement or its grid changes while you review, you'll see **This page changed after the scan. Close this and scan again. Nothing was added.** Cancelling saves nothing. Accepted changes save on the device first and sync when online, keeping the same pocket and copy IDs through retries and reloads.

## Offline

Cached startup is read-only, so reconnect and let pending edits finish before scanning. A scanner that is already open keeps its cached lookup if the network drops, but adding cards needs a complete online read. It can't identify uncached cards or download the text reader for the first time offline.

## API

There is no scan endpoint. The review submits the same `execute_inventory_commands` contract available at `POST /app/api/v1/storage/inventory/commands` and over MCP, using `insertAssign` for new pockets.
