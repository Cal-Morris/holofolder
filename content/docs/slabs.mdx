---
title: Slabs view
description: Provider-specific holder previews for graded copies in a collection.
---

Open a collection and choose **Slabs** in the view picker (next to **Binder** and **List**). The Slabs view shows each copy in the collection that has saved grading, using its owned-copy record. PSA, supported current and historic CGC, and Beckett silver, gold and black each have their own holder and label layout. A grade of 10 alone never selects Black Label or Pristine. Raw copies stay raw. Unknown holder versions keep their entered metadata in a neutral holder.

No provider import or slab photo is needed: the preview uses the catalog card image plus the recorded provider and grade. Missing catalog images show a readable fallback. Exact ACE/custom artwork and unsupported historical labels are shown as unavailable. Custom labels use the recorded text with one of four neutral colors. No certificate, barcode, QR code, verification graphic, invented grade or estimated slab value is added.

The gallery and enlarged detail are read-only. View, search and scroll position are saved on this device for the signed-in account and collection owner. When the app has started from saved data offline, the gallery is read-only and may be incomplete until it refreshes. Switching views never moves or regrades a copy. To change grading, use [manual grading](/docs/manual-grading) in Inventory.

Certificates, private record links and original label or slab assets are never passed to the renderer or shown on the page. There is no public slab sharing: public binder links show ordinary card artwork with recorded provider and grade text only.

The view adds no REST or MCP operation. Developers read grading through the [native reads](/docs/native-reads), which return the redacted public projection.
