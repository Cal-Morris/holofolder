---
title: Page art
description: Lay one picture across several pockets or a whole binder page, with your cards on top, and print it to size.
---

Page art lets a binder page read as one picture. You lay a picture behind a group of pockets, from a single pocket up to the whole page, and the empty pockets under it join together and show their part of the picture. Your cards sit on top in their own pockets, and when you take a card out, the picture shows through again. Collectors call this the Michi method.

## Add a picture to a page

1. Go to the page, tap **⋮** beside the binder name and choose **Edit layout**.
2. If you like, tap the pockets you want the picture to cover.
3. Tap **⋯** (More options), then **Page art on page** followed by the page number.
4. On the small page, drag across the pockets the picture should cover, or tap two opposite corners.
5. Choose a picture:
   - **Card** to use any card's picture. Search by name, set or number.
   - **Pokémon** to use a Pokémon's official artwork.
   - **Your image** to use your own picture, such as fan art or a scan. It can be a PNG, JPEG or WebP under 5 MB, and only you, your partner and people you share the binder with can see it.
6. Choose **Fill and crop** or **Fit whole picture**, then use **Zoom**, **Left–right** and **Up–down** to frame it.
7. Tap **Save page art**.

A page can have several pictures as long as they don't overlap. A new picture that overlaps one already on the page replaces it. To change a picture, open page art again and tap it on the small page. To take one away, tap it and choose **Remove**.

The picture belongs to the pocket at its top-left corner, so it moves with your pockets when you move a page or add pages. If you remove that pocket, its picture goes too.

## Print it for your real binder

You can print the picture cut into card-sized pieces to slide into the pockets of your real binder.

1. Open page art for the page and scroll to **Print**.
2. Choose **A4** or **US Letter**.
3. Measure the gap in your binder from the edge of one sleeved card to the edge of the next, and type it into **Gap between pockets (mm)**. The print leaves that strip out, so once the pieces are in their pockets the picture lines up across the seams. Leave it at 0 if you'd rather the picture be cut edge to edge.
4. Leave **Skip pockets that hold a card** on to print only the pockets still waiting for a card.
5. Tap **Print this page's art**, and print at 100% ("Actual size") with scaling turned off.

Each piece is 63 × 88 mm, the size of a card, with thin marks to cut along. The first sheet has a 50 mm line you can measure to check the size came out right. Every piece has a small "PAGE ART" label so it can't be mistaken for a real card.

Card and Pokémon pictures are fairly small, so across a large area they print a little soft. Your own image prints sharper.

## Shares and offline

People who open your [shared binder](/help/sharing) see your page art, including your own images. Page art also shows when you're offline once the binder has been opened on your device. Adding or changing a picture needs a connection.
